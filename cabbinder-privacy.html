Cab Binder for iPhone

Privacy Policy

Effective September 22, 2026
Short version: your documents and dates never leave your phone. The app has no account, no GPS, no analytics, and no ads. Purchases go through Apple.

What we collect

Nothing. Cab Binder does not ask for your name, email, phone number, or location, and it does not transmit your drivers, trucks, compliance items, document photos, USDOT or MC numbers, or settings to us or to anyone else.

Where your data lives

Every record — driver and truck profiles, expiration dates, renewal history, document photos, business details, and your settings — is stored on your iPhone in the app's private storage. If your device backs up to iCloud, Apple may include that data in your encrypted device backup under Apple's terms. We have no access to it.

Photos and camera

If you photograph a document (a medical card, CDL, cab card, insurance card, permit), the app asks for access to the camera or to the specific photo you pick at that moment. The image is stored inside the app on your device and is never uploaded. You can delete any photo at any time.

Face ID

If you turn on the app lock, Cab Binder uses Face ID or your passcode through Apple's Local Authentication framework to unlock the app. The app never sees or stores your biometric data; Apple handles it entirely on the device.

Location

The app never requests or uses Location Services. There is no GPS or background tracking of any kind.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules local notifications ahead of each expiration on your device. Nothing about it is sent to a server.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. Apple's handling of purchase information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not use any third-party purchase or analytics service.

Exports and sharing

When you export a roadside binder PDF or a CSV, the file is created on your device and handed to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes after that — Mail, Files, AirDrop — is your choice. Show-to-officer mode only displays the document on your screen; it transmits nothing.

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets

The widgets read the same on-device data as the app, through a private App Group container. Nothing is shared outside your device.

Analytics and advertising

The app contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all data removes every entry and photo immediately. Deleting the app also removes all of its data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Not an official credential or legal advice

Cab Binder is a personal record generated from your own entries. It is not an official credential and not legal advice. Default renewal intervals are general guidance drawn from published federal rules (for example 49 CFR Parts 382, 391 and 396) and agency guidance; you can change any of them. Many states accept electronic images of credentials; carry originals where required. Verify current rules with FMCSA and your base jurisdiction.
