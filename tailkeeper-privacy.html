Tailkeeper for iPhone

Privacy Policy

Effective September 22, 2026
Short version: your pets' records never leave your phone. The app has no account, no GPS, no analytics, and no ads. Purchases go through Apple.

What we collect

Nothing. Tailkeeper does not ask for your name, email, phone number, or location, and it does not transmit your pets' profiles, vaccine and vet records, photos, or settings to us or to anyone else.

Where your data lives

Every record — pet profiles, vaccine history, microchip details, vet visits, medications, attached photos, and your settings — is stored on your iPhone in the app's private storage. If your device backs up to iCloud, Apple may include that data in your encrypted device backup under Apple's terms. We have no access to it.

Photos and camera

If you attach a photo (a pet picture, a rabies certificate, microchip paperwork), the app asks for access to the specific photo you pick or the camera at that moment. The image is stored inside the app on your device and is never uploaded.

Location

The app never requests or uses Location Services. There is no GPS or background tracking of any kind.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules local notifications for due vaccines, check-ups, and medications on your device. Nothing about it is sent to a server.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. Apple's handling of purchase information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not use any third-party purchase or analytics service.

Exports and sharing

When you export a vaccine record PDF or microchip card, the file is created on your device and handed to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes after that — Mail, Files, AirDrop — is your choice.

Home Screen widget

The widget reads the same on-device data as the app, through a private App Group container. Nothing is shared outside your device.

Analytics and advertising

The app contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all data removes every entry immediately. Deleting the app also removes all of its data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Not veterinary advice

The built-in vaccine schedules are general guidance based on published AAHA and AAFP recommendations. They are not veterinary advice, and your veterinarian's schedule and local rabies laws take precedence. The vaccine record you export contains owner-entered information; confirm with your clinic's documentation where proof is required.

Support

Email blainealamb@gmail.com with the app version (Settings → About) and a short description. Most questions are answered within two business days. To restore a purchase on a new phone, open the paywall from Settings → Tailkeeper Pro and tap Restore purchases.
